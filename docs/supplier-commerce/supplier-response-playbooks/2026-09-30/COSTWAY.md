## What Costway confirmed

1. Costway operates a dropship programme, but cooperation/application must be confirmed first.
2. After cooperation is confirmed they can provide a CSV updated once daily.
3. Products that are in stock in the CSV may be sold, subject to their programme rules.
4. Costway brand name, logo and other brand assets must not be used on our website/listings/marketing/sales channels unless separately agreed in advance.
5. Retail prices must not be below Costway's official UK retail price; if a listing is below their official price, they require adjustment.
6. Orders are placed through their B2B `promotion.com` platform using an order template / Excel upload route.
7. Invoices and tracking are available through the B2B platform.
8. Outer packaging is SKU/barcode based, contains no Costway branding and no invoice in the parcel.
9. Warranty follows Costway website terms and they supplied a customer-service route for after-sales support.
10. They stated there is no additional fee for the described order-template process.
