# Cosmetic Plus — Supplier Playbook

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02  
Gmail thread: `1a0d0899bee991c0`  
Current classification: **REJECTED FOR CURRENT SUPPLIER COMMERCE MODEL**

## What the supplier confirmed

Cosmetic Plus confirmed:
- trade-account based direct-to-customer fulfilment;
- each order is placed and paid through its trade portal;
- trade prices are ex VAT and payment is upfront;
- a dropship parcel charge applies;
- logged-in accounts can access CSV/XML containing barcode, brand, product name, quantity and price;
- UK-only fulfilment;
- plain unbranded parcels;
- dispatch target of 48 hours;
- defined damaged/faulty/wrong-item handling.

## Blocking issues

The supplier explicitly stated that it:
- cannot give written marketplace permission for specific brands/SKUs;
- has no authority from brands to grant official brand imagery, descriptions or marketing rights;
- cannot provide documentation tracing stock back to the brand;
- leaves marketplace-channel, pricing and compliance rules to the reseller.

For beauty/cosmetics, Loadify requires stronger product-rights, provenance and compliance evidence before publication.
## Decision

Do not import Cosmetic Plus products into Loadify Market under the current controlled pilot.

Do not treat CSV/XML availability as publication approval.

Do not use supplier website imagery as a substitute for verified brand/content rights.

Reconsider only if future evidence independently closes:
- marketplace/channel permission;
- image/content rights;
- brand/product provenance;
- authenticity traceability;
- applicable cosmetics/fragrance compliance;
- product-specific restrictions and pricing rules.

No further supplier email is required at this stage.
