# Loadify Market Romania — GDPR Article 27 EU Representative Assessment
Date: 2026-09-28
Status: PRE-LAUNCH blocker

## Official legal basis
GDPR Article 27 requires a controller or processor not established in the Union to designate in writing a representative in the Union where Article 3(2) applies.

The exception is narrow: the processing must be occasional, must not include large-scale special-category/criminal-offence processing, and must be unlikely to result in risk to individuals, taking into account nature, context, scope and purposes.

Official sources:
- GDPR Article 27: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- EDPB Guidelines 3/2018 on territorial scope: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en

## Loadify facts evidenced by current product design
- XDrive Logistics Ltd is established in the United Kingdom, not in an EU Member State.
- Romania is a deliberately targeted marketplace, not an incidental one-off transaction route.
- The Romania product design includes account creation, orders, payments, delivery addresses, seller onboarding, returns, support, messages, notifications and analytics/consent infrastructure.
- The intended processing is therefore recurring as part of an ongoing marketplace service if Romania goes live.

## Assessment
Article 3(2) is expected to apply to the Romania offering because the service is intentionally offered to people in the EU.

The Article 27(2)(a) exception is **not assumed**. The intended Romania processing is part of a continuing marketplace operation and is not designed as merely occasional processing.

Accordingly, the engineering/legal launch posture is:
1. Romania remains PRE-LAUNCH.
2. Before commercial launch, XDrive Logistics Ltd must either:
   - designate in writing an EU representative satisfying Article 27 and publish the representative's contact details in the Romania privacy information; or
   - retain a documented, supportable legal basis establishing that the Article 27 exception actually applies.
3. No representative identity may be invented or inferred.
4. The existing launch/compliance gate must remain fail-closed until verified evidence is recorded.

## Representative location
Article 27(3) requires the representative to be established in one of the Member States where relevant data subjects are located in relation to the offering/monitoring. For a Romania-focused launch, a representative established in Romania would satisfy that geographic condition; another Member State could only be used where the relevant Article 27(3) condition is also met.

## Evidence required to close
- written representative appointment/mandate;
- representative legal name and EU address;
- operational contact channel;
- privacy notice update;
- internal record identifying the Article 27 mandate and effective date;
- verified compliance-evidence record for the Romania launch gate.

If the company instead relies on the exception, retain the written assessment and evidence proving that each element of Article 27(2)(a) is met.
