Definition of Complete for the Future /super-admin

This is the checklist that prevents us from calling the control plane finished too early.

BEFORE PRODUCTION WIRING
1. Authority
Dedicated Platform Owner authority, delegation model, re-auth / high-risk approval rules and no privilege leakage.
2. Truth
Every number maps to canonical order, finance, tax, seller, supplier and reconciliation truth; no parallel ledgers.
3. Coverage
Marketplace, B2B/services, logistics, growth, trust, privacy, Supplier Commerce and platform operations all represented.
4. Safe Mutations
Server-owned endpoints, RLS/service-role boundaries, idempotency, audit, confirmation and rollback/recovery.
5. Live Health
Netlify, Supabase, Stripe, notifications, supplier runtime, monitoring and recovery probes are real, not decorative.
6. Evidence Gates
Provider capability, Shadow/pilot, tax/compliance, supplier rights and recovery evidence fail closed when absent/stale.
7. E2E
Buyer/Seller/Admin/Owner flows, money paths, disputes/returns, shipping, audit and permission denials tested end-to-end.
8. Owner Review
Desktop/mobile visual audit, accessibility, performance, density and action hierarchy approved before main.
Architecture decision represented here: `/admin` remains the operational Admin Hub. The future `/super-admin` is a higher Platform Owner control plane spanning canonical marketplace truth, global money/risk/compliance, Supplier Commerce, autonomy, security/audit, monitoring and recovery. The current repository does not yet model a dedicated Platform Owner authority distinct from ordinary Admin, so no production mutation should be attached to this screen until that boundary is separately designed, implemented and security-reviewed.
