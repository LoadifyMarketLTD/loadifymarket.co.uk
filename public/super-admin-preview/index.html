Design
Architecture decision shown in this preview: `/admin` remains the operational Admin Hub. The future `/super-admin` becomes a Platform Owner control plane above it: global health, money/risk truth, Supplier Commerce governance, security/audit and high-impact decisions. A dedicated owner authority model should be designed and security-reviewed separately before any production mutations are attached to this screen.
